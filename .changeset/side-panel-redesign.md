Redesign the side panel. Each status group has GitHub's own icon, and each pull request shows its reviewers' profile pictures ringed by their verdict, with a dashed ring for reviews still owed. Review requests show who opened them, mark a review asked for again, and show your own verdict once given. Merge conflicts and new comments are flagged. The side panel and settings follow the system's light or dark mode, and the arrow keys move between tabs.
